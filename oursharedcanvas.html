Introduction and Background

Our second major project in our media and tech class was centered around the use of art to adress social issues. We all attempted to target specific issues with our individual screenprinted artworks, and associated short films about our art processes. We later sewed together our artworks, to unify and strengthen our messages. Throughout this project, we used a variety of physical and digital tools to design and create our art, such as Loopy, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Sillhouette Studio, CAMEO Machines, Adobe Audition, and Adobe Premier Pro.

My Art and Targeted Social Issue

\

I chose to adress global clean water scarcity/shortage. I depicted this issue with a landscape scene of a hand clutching a water bottle, reaching out of the ocean infront of a post-apocalyptic ruined city. Above this, I included the headlining text "AD NAUSEAM" (meaning "to sickness" in latin), to draw attention to the unnecessity of, and relatively easy solution to the issue.
