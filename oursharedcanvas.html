Our Shared Canvas

Introduction and Background

Our second major project in our media and tech class was centered around the use of art to adress social issues. We all attempted to target specific issues with our individual screenprinted artworks, and associated short films about our art processes. We chose specific small and large audiences to target with our art—from our families to our city and beyond. We later sewed together our artworks, to unify and strengthen our messages. To complete these goals, we used a variety of physical and digital tools, such as Loopy, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Silhouette Studio and Cutting Machines, Adobe Audition, and Adobe Premiere.

My Art and Targeted Social Issue

I chose to adress global clean water scarcity/shortage. I depicted this issue with a landscape scene of a hand clutching a water bottle, reaching out of the ocean infront of a post-apocalyptic ruined city. Above this, I included the headlining text "AD NAUSEAM" (meaning "to sickness" in latin), to draw attention to the unnecessity of, and relatively easy solution to the issue.

Acquired and Practiced Skill Reflection

Throughout this project, and throughout all of the individual processes I had to go through in order to create my own screenprinted artwork and my short film about it, I learned so much in so many broad and specific catagories, especially in the way of useful 21st Century Skills. Most foremost though, I believe that this project helped me to progress in the way of my creativity and innovation. In every step of the process, I was forced to work creatively: designing my own art, using new and different strategies to bring my art into reality, solving problems with my own methods and direction, and finding a way to quickly and effectively present it all in a short film. There is no doubt in my mind that this project also significantly developed other such skills, such as communication, collaboration, initiative, self direction, and more.
